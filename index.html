About

Whatever the format, the goal doesn't change.

I got into editing the same way most people do — cutting clips for fun, then realizing every part of it, pacing, sound, color, could be pushed further. That curiosity is why I never settled into one niche.

Today I work across formats: fast-paced shorts, clean talking-head content, narrative long-form, and brand pieces that need to sell without feeling like an ad. Each one has its own rhythm, and switching between them is the part of the job I enjoy most.
